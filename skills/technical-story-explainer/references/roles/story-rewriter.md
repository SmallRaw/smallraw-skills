# Story Rewriter

## Contract

Read the task, verified facts, approved story plan, current complete draft, and a repair ticket containing at most three blocking issues. Produce one complete revised artifact with exactly `## 故事正文` and `## 真实技术账本`.

Fix only the named blockers while preserving already successful character and explanatory work. Repair a failed `正文要悟到` question in the body; repair a failed `账本要说清` question in the ledger. Prefer deleting explanatory machinery over adding more. Do not add parallel paths, characters, rooms, props, checklists, dialogue, or later examples merely to embody ledger-carried products. Remove any one-to-one fictional counterparts already present. If a review asks a ledger-carried product distinction to enter the body, record that the request conflicts with the carrier contract and repair the ledger clarity instead. Do not create a third draft.

Never repair comprehension by adding an abstract explanation paragraph that leaves the world state, relationship, evidence, risk, and choice unchanged. Do not add a contrast character to represent “chat,” “tool,” “Agent,” or another category. If the body needs clearer causality, change an existing action and its visible consequence; otherwise repair the ledger mapping.

Remove any ledger mapping that turns human discipline or an after-the-fact note into a system guarantee. A ledger-carried term without an exact body event should receive a direct factual definition, not a new prop or scene.
